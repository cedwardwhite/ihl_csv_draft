Description:
This print illustrates the proverb "bad news travels quickly" or "bad news has wings." (Lit.: bad new runs one thousand "ri" [about 2.44 miles], a traditional measure of distance in Japan.)
These very popular prints were issued as part of the series Kyōsai hyakuzu consisting of more than 200 pictorializations of proverbs and depictions of famous subjects unrelated to proverbs. The prints were published continuously as single-sheet prints from c. 1863 to 1866 by the publisher Wakasaya Yoichi and the works were later reprinted in album form by the publisher Ōkura Magobei (Ōkuraya) in 1881 and 1886. Many different color schemes were employed in the various editions and reprintings. Most of the prints in this collection are likely from one of Ōkura Magobei's later reprint editions, as they show evidence of their original album backings.
