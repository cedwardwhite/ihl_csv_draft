Description:
From Senpan's fourth set of prints picturing Japanese onsens. This print depicts a gentleman in a trench coat sitting with two traditionally dressed women on the rocks overlooking the Yu-no-hama hot springs resort along the coast of Yamagata. To quote from a current-day advertisement about these hot springs:
"Warming yourself up with a soak in this hot spring after swimming in the ocean and playing water sports on a beach of white sand and clear blue skies is a truly unique experience. Because this hot spring is along the ocean, the seafood is exquisite. After you’re done enjoying the hot spring, you can have a romantic evening eating delicious seafood while watching the sun set into the ocean."
ABOUT THE SERIES HOT SPRING NOTES
One of his most famous series of prints, "Woodblock Prints, Hot Spring Notes" (Hanga yokusen fu), depicts various hot springs around Japan. It was published in five volumes of twenty prints each over an 18 year period, starting in 1941 when Maekawa was evacuated to Okayama in southwest Honshu and ending in 1959.
Merritt states that most of the prints were started during the war but could not be published until the war was over. She notes that although his countrymen are depicted at one of their favorite pastimes, spa going for rest and relaxation, "the feeling is not joyous," due to Maekawa's use of somber colors. The general demeanor of the spa goers is also somber in many of the prints.
However, writing in 1955, Oliver Statler found Senpan's spa prints "some of his pleasantest prints" and praised Senpan's "connoisseur's selection from the country's hundreds of hot spring resorts."
In 1954 the publisher Ryūseikaku 竜星閣 released a volume containing full size reproductions of the sixty woodblock prints contained in the first three volumes.
The five volumes comprising the entire series are titled are:
Woodblock Prints, Hot Spring Notes (版画 浴泉譜 Hanga yokusen fu), 1941.
More Woodblock Prints, Hot Spring Notes (続版画 浴泉譜 Zoku hanga yokusen fu), 1944.
More and More Woodblock Prints, Hot Spring Notes (続続版画 浴泉譜 Zoku zoku hanga yokusen fu), 1952.
More, More, and More Woodblock Prints, Hot Spring Notes (続続続版画 浴泉譜 Zoku, zoku, zoku yokusen fu), 1956.
More, More, More, and More Woodblock Prints, Hot Spring Notes (続続続続版画 浴泉譜 Zoku, zoku, zoku, zoku hanga yokusen fu), 1959.
