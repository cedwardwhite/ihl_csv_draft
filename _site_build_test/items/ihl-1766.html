Narrative Date:
Originally issued 1931 - 昭和六年作 (a work of Shōwa 6th year.) This collection's print a later edition bearing the round 6mm diameter Watanabe "A-type" seal, used by the publisher between 1946 and 1957.
Earliest Date of Creation or Publication:
1931
Description:
Originally issued in 1931, this collection's print is a later edition c. 1946-1957.
"Less successful are Koson's attempts at integrated landscape compositions such as "Mallards in a coastal scene," one of the few dated prints by the artist (1931), in which the groupings of ducks appear wooden and stolid in an equally lackluster landscape setting. The forms of the diving and swimming ducks in this print are clearly borrowed from other prints like "Mallards and lotus" or visa versa. In Japanese art, the reuse of motifs and the reworking of compositions by painters and print designers is not unusual . . . ." - Source: Crows, Cranes & Camellias: The Natural World of Ohara Koson 1877-1945, Amy Reigle Newland, Jan Perrée, Robert Schaap, Hotei Publishing, 2003, plate 146 caption.
