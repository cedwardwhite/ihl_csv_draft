Title:
Evacuees in the Suburbs of Tokyo (東京郊外の避難者 Tokyo kōgai no hinansha)
Series:
Color Woodblock Picture Album of the History of the Taishō Great Earthquake (木版色摺 大正大震史畫譜 Taishō daishinshi gafu mokuhan irozurie)
Creator:
Fukunaga Seihan 福永晴帆 (1884-1961)
Narrative Date:
1923 (Taishō 12)
Earliest Date of Creation or Publication:
1923
Description:
A poignant scene of young children and their mothers camping on railroad tracks on the outskirts of Tokyo following the 1923 Great Kantō Earthquake. A single soldier stands guard as the moon rises. The quake was so violent that even the railroad tracks were bent by its force.
