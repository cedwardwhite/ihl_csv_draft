Title:
Hasedera - temple 8 (長谷寺 八)
Series:
Picture Album of the Thirty-Three Pilgrimage Places of the Western Provinces (西国三十三所巡礼画巻 Saigoku sanjūsansho junrei gakan)
Creator:
Nakazawa Hiromitsu 中沢弘光 (1874-1964)
Narrative Date:
September 28, 1925 大正十四年九月十八日発行 as printed in the 1925 album colophon
Earliest Date of Creation or Publication:
1925
Description:
Nakazawa has us viewing Hase-dera, the eight temple on the Saigoku Kannon Pilgrimage, from the vantage point of an inn next to the Yamato River east of Osaka. We see the hondō, main building up on the slopes of Mount Hatsuse.
Along with his two traveling companions, the poet Ishikura Suiyō and the artist Akatsuka Chūichi, Nakazawa undertook this 78 day 600 mile pilgrimage in December 1923, only three months after the Great Kantō Earthquake devastated the Tokyo area.
This print is from the complete 1925 first edition portfolio (IHL.2413, IHL.2413.01-IHL.2413.59) titled Saigoku sanjūsansho junrei gakan (Picture Album of the Thirty-Three Pilgrimage Places of the Western Provinces) published by Kanao Tanejirō featuring 37 woodblock prints made from Nakazawa's sketches, supplemented with poetry and information about the temples.
