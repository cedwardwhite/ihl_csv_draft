Title:
The Huge Win of the Armed Forces, No. 4 appearing in Kagoshima-ken ari no sonomama (鹿児島県有のそのまま・四号・はら切坂友軍大勝利)
Series:
Creator:
Hasegawa Sadanobu II 二代 長谷川貞信 (Konobu I 初代小信) (1848-1940) - attributed to the artist
Narrative Date:
Printed and Published* March 5, 1877 (Meiji 10) - 明治十年三月五日 御届 出版 as printed in lower left of yellow scroll.
* 御届 - notification delivered of intent to publish (otodoke)
Earliest Date of Creation or Publication:
1877
Description:
The fourth print in a series of twenty prints describing and depicting the 1877 Satsuma Rebellion published under the newspaper-like banner Kagoshima ken ari no sonomama, loosely translated as "Kagoshima Prefecture As It Is Now." Published in Osaka by Kanai Tokubei 金井徳兵衛, the prints, classified as nishiki-e shinbun (newspaper prints), provided short articles with accompanying simplified graphics of the Rebellion's progress, or lack thereof. All twenty prints in the series show a publication month of March or April, so the life of this "newspaper" was even shorter than the nine month rebellion. For another print published under this banner see IHL.0567
The red cartouche in the upper right hand corner reads 鹿児嶋縣 (Kagoshima-ken) 有のそのまま (Ari no sonomama) and also provides the print number in the series, 四 (4). The large print seen on the far right in the yellow scroll reads はら切坂友軍大勝利, a loose translation of which is "the huge win of the armed forces at Harakirizaka [Tawarazaka]." The red cartouches in the center of the print identify the rider on the horse as Major General Miyoshi Shigetomo, the former commander of the Osaka Brigade.
No artist's signature or seal appears on any of the twenty prints, and neither Waseda University or Kagawa University Library list the artist, but two sources, Andreas Marks on p. 190 of his book "Japanese Woodblock Prints: Artists, Publishers and Masterworks 1680-1900" and Kansai University Library attribute the prints to Hasegawa Sadanobu II (Konobu I) (1848-1940).
The entire series of twenty prints may be seen on the Waseda University Library website at http://archive.wul.waseda.ac.jp/kosho/bunko10/bunko10_08061/ [accessed 8-17-23]
