Title:
Bandō Takesaburō, Iwai Kumesaburō and Onoe Kikujirō in the play Sekai no hana Oguri gaiden (坂東竹三郎、岩井粂三郎、尾上菊次郎・世界花小栗外伝)
Creator:
Utagawa Kunisada I 歌川国貞 (1786–1865)
Narrative Date:
1851 - Mera 米良 and Watanabe 渡辺 double nanushi censor seals of 1851.
Earliest Date of Creation or Publication:
1851
Description:
From right to left, the actors Bandō Takesaburō as Oguri Sōtan (actually Kaneuji), Iwai Kumesaburō III as Manchō's daughter (musume) Okoma, and Onoe Kikujirō II As Kohagi (actually princess Terute) in the play Sekai no hana Oguri gaiden (Worldly Flowers, an Oguri Anecdote), performed at Edo's Nakamura Theatre 中村 in the 4th month of 1851. This print is part of an untitled series of horizontal actor prints, created c. 4th month of 1851 by the artist.
The shita-uri シタ売 seal is present on this collection's print indicating a print not allowed to be displayed under censorship rules and that was sold "under the counter."
