Title:
Tsuru no chitose Soga no kadomatsu (鶴千歳曽我門松)
Creator:
Utagawa Yoshiiku 歌川芳幾 (1833-1904)
Narrative Date:
February 1865 - aratame censor seal reading 丑二改
Earliest Date of Creation or Publication:
1865
Description:
Depicting the six actors (from right to left) Ichimura Kakitsu IV 市村家橘, Ichikawa Shinsha 市川新車, Nakamura Tsuruzō 中村鶴蔵, Bandō Hikosaburō V 坂東彦三郎, Onoe Kikujirō II 尾上菊次郎 and Sawamura Tosshō II 沢村訥升 in this Soga Brothers themed New Year's play of revenge titled Tsuru no chitose Soga no kadomatsu. For another print depicting this play see IHL.0954.
