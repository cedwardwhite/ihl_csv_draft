Title:
View of the West Gate of Shitennō-ji Temple (四天王寺西門の景)

Creator:
Hayashi Motoharu 林基春 (1858-1903)

Printed April 5, 1895 - 明治廿八年四月五日印刷 as printed in left margin; Published April 18, 1895 - 同四月一八日発行 as printed in left margin.
Earliest Date of Creation or Publication:
1895
Description:
Visitors in traditional and Western dress near the stone torii leading to the West Gate (Gokuraku mon) of Shitennnōji, Japan's first Buddhist temple, located in Osaka. The five-storied pagoda (Gojūnotō) can be seen in the background. The yellow sign pillar to the right of the torii reads 従是東公園地 大阪府, telling us that we are at the affiliated East Park area in Osaka prefecture.
This portfolio of color lithographs 色石版画, issued by the Osaka publisher Furushima Takejirō in 1895, consists of ten well-known scenes in Kyoto and ten scenes in Osaka. The portfolio is likely the earliest use of color lithography, most likely using zinc plates, for the production of meisho-e, or pictures of famous places. The portfolio was sold in shops in the exposition hall and in souvenir/gift shops around Kyoto. The selling price and number of total copies is unknown, but it appears that at least two editions of this portfolio were produced. As over 1.3 million people attended the exposition, the print runs must have been quite large.
