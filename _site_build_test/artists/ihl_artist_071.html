Kojima Shōgetsu 小島勝月 (active c. 1870–1904)

Kojima Shgetsu 3 (active c. 18701904) According to Woodblock Kuchi-e Prints: Reflections of Meiji Culture, Kojima Shgetsu was born Kojima Katsumi and used theg Tshū 2, which appears on many of his prints. He lived in the Asakusa area of Tokyo and painted genre scenes andsashi-efor several newspapers and magazines including Miyako no hana.
