Biography

Yukawa Hiromitsu 湯川広光 (active c. 1894)

No information seems to be available about this artist who was active around 1894.  In addition to the two Sino-Japanese War (1894-1895 ) senso-e in this collection (which are part of a twelve print series by the artist) the Database of Japanese Art in Overseas Collections shows Moscow's Pushkin State Museum of Fine Arts having a number of undated fan prints by the artist (see sample below) in their collection.
