Biography

Utagawa Masanobu 歌川 政信 (fl. 1882-1887)
A fifth generation Utagawa school artist 1 about whom nothing is known.  He did create several prints of the circus, as pictured below, and at least one series of prints depicting Tokyo meisho (famous places.)  He used the g ō Baido 梅童 for some of his works.

1 http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Utagawa_school_members
