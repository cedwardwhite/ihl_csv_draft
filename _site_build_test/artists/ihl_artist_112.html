Ogata Gekkō 尾形月耕 (1859-1920)

Source: Conflicts of Interest: Art and War in Modern Japan , Philip K. Hu, et. al., Saint Louis Museum of Art, 2016, p. 229. A Japanese style ( nihonga ) painter and print designer, Harada is best known for his prints of the Russo-Japanese War (1904-1905), both woodblock prints and lithographs. A student of Ogata Gekkō (1859-1920) , his master’s style is evident in his woodblock prints. During the late 1800s he exhibited paintings of historical subjects at Tokyo venues including the Third Japan Youth Painting Promotion Society Exhibition 日本青年絵画共進会 of 1895, the exhibition of the organization that his master co-founded. * also seen as 原田耕舉 Examples of the Artist’s Work Two Woodblock Prints of the Russo-Japanese War Our Destroyers Hayatori and Asagiri Sinking Enemy Ships at Port Arthur During a Great Snowstorm at 3:00 a.m. on February 14, 1904 Museum of Fine Arts, Boston 2000.543a-c Our Destroyers Fiercely Fought the Enemy Ships Outside of Port Arthur and Defeated the Enemy Fleet at Dawn April 13, 1904 Museum of Fine Arts, Boston 2000.455a-c A Scroll Attributed to the Artist Tora , undated signed and sealed: 耕挙 Kōkyo Sample Signatures and Seals of the Artist 耕挙 / 耕 Kōkyo with Kō seal 耕挙 / 耕 舉 Kōkyo with Kō kyo seal 耕挙画 / ?
