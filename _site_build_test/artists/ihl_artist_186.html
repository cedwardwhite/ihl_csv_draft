Utagawa Yoshiiku 歌川芳幾 (1833-1904)

Source: The Hotei Encyclopedia of Japanese Woodblock Prints, Amy Reigle Newland, Hotei Publishing Company, 2005, p.505. Tokyo print designer. A student of Utagawa Yoshiiku (1833-1904), he produced a number of prints dealing with manners and customs, famous views ( meish ō ), as well as war prints and kabuki actor prints. Other gagō: Hikitei, Shinbitei. Name sometimes seen as Utagawa 歌川 Ikuhide 幾英
